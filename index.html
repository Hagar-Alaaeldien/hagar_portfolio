About me

An engineering background applied to software quality, with a focus on clear testing, practical investigation, and reliable system behavior.

I am a Communication & Information Engineering graduate from the University of Science and Technology at Zewail City and an ISTQB®-certified Junior QA Engineer. My testing experience covers manual test case design and execution, defect reporting, functional and integration testing, API workflows, database validation, and Agile methodologies.

I have also worked on systems that combine software with Raspberry Pi, Arduino, sensors, mobile applications, AI models, and cloud services. This engineering background helps me approach testing from both the user and system perspectives.

I am particularly interested in software testing roles where careful validation, clear communication, and structured problem solving can contribute to reliable software.
